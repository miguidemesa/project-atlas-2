# Design System: Atlas

> The normative source is the Atlas Design System artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/3hHGXiAueSbNMkYBhzD5Z2), with every screen in Atlas Screens (https://claude.ai/artifact/WyYV9f9YJkaB67Df6SFRGf). The Next.js app adopts this system in P2. Until then the frontend code still runs the phase 0 to 3 theme, so read tokens from here, not from `globals.css`.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "A Game Store After Dark"**

Atlas is where collectors in the Philippines buy and sell every trading card game, priced in pesos. It looks like a game store at night. The surfaces are night navy, the text is starlight, and holo cyan is the one action colour. Light only exists where it hits a card: a tilted card catches a sweep of holographic foil, and nothing else in the interface shines. The layout uses the conventions collectors already know from game stores: a two-tier header with a store bar, a left rail, a featured capsule with thumbnails, deals, ranked lists and category capsules. Atlas takes that structure and never the look. The colours, type, foil and honesty rules are its own.

The density is that of a daily store. Desktop pairs a 220px rail with a wide main column of five-up shelves and ranked lists. Phones are thumb-first: a search pill with swipeable store chips, a swipeable carousel where the next capsule peeks in, sideways shelves, and a tab bar that sits on the safe area. Card photos are the heroes. They are never cropped, never recoloured, and always shown with their condition or grade up front.

Every game keeps its own colour wherever it appears, as a dot beside its name. Its hub wears its full skin. Prices come from real paid sales on Atlas, and a deal is measured against the 30-day median, never against a struck-through "was" price. The user confirmed two rejections: anything too close to a copy of Steam, and game colours getting lost.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Night navy surfaces layered by tone, with hairline borders at rest.
- One holo-cyan action colour, and foil kept for cards.
- Unbounded headings and prices, Onest text, JetBrains Mono data.
- Store structure: two-tier header, rail, featured carousel, price ticker, deals, ranked lists, game capsules.
- A colour line for every game, and full skins on game hubs.
- One authored motion (the holo tilt), and two movers (carousel and ticker) that can always be paused.
- Phone-first touch: 44px targets, swipe tracks, peeking shelves.

## Colors

A night palette: navy surfaces and starlight ink, one holo-cyan action, foil that stays on the cards, and a colour line for every game.

### Primary
- **Holo Cyan** (#40e2e7): the only action colour. It fills primary buttons, focus rings, unread dots, the Sell tab, the current step, and the selected booth and map pin. Hover lifts to **Bright Cyan** (#7aeef1). Text and icons on it are **Deep Teal Ink** (#04181a, 11.5:1).
- **Cyan Wash** (#0b2126) with **Cyan Line** (rgba(64,226,231,0.26)): Atlas speaking for itself, in the announcement bar and the sell band.

### Secondary
- **Holographic Foil**: Ice (#7cf2ff), Lilac (#b39cff), Rose (#ff8fd6) and Gold (#ffd27a), in that order. They appear in exactly two places: the light that sweeps across a tilted card photo (blended with `color-dodge`), and the fill of the carousel's current progress bar.

### Tertiary
- **Game Colour Lines**: each game's hub accent, worn as a dot beside its name in tiles, rows, the ticker, the rail, the carousel, event tickets and menus. Pokémon is yellow (#f5cd47), One Piece red (#ff7a66), Basketball blue (#8fb3f5), Lorcana violet (#c9a7f2), Yu-Gi-Oh! pink (oklch(0.84 0.12 330)) and Magic peach (oklch(0.84 0.12 35)). Every other game derives its accent as oklch(0.84 0.12 h) from its hue. On a hub, the eleven `--skin-*` roles re-colour the page; cards and wells never take a skin.

### Status
- **Mint** (#48d597): good news. NM condition, prices up, paid and released steps, verified shields, the Under market deal.
- **Sky** (#8ab4ff): information. LP condition, held money, info notices, your location.
- **Amber** (#f3b65b): time and caution. MP condition, ending soon, event pins.
- **Coral** (#ff8a8a): losses and problems. Prices down, HP condition, errors, outbid, disputes.
- **Slab Gold** (#e6c57f): graded slabs and star ratings only.
- Each has a matching line colour for chip and notice borders. Every status colour comes with a word or an icon.

### Neutral
- **Night Navy** (#0c121e): the page ground outside game hubs.
- **Night Panel** (#121a29): panels, menus, inputs, the store bar and order summaries.
- **Night Raised** (#18233a): hovered and selected rows, booths, received chat bubbles.
- **Chrome** (#090e18): the global bar, the ticker band, the phone tab bar and sticky phone action bars.
- **Photo Stage** (#0a0f1a) and **Card Well** (#0e1524, line #1f2a3d): the stage behind card photos, and the 63:88 box every card photo sits in.
- **Starlight Ink** (#eef3ff, 17:1 on navy) for primary text, **Soft Ink** (#cdd6ea) for bright secondary text, **Dim Ink** (#9eabc6, 8.1:1) for body and meta, **Faint Ink** (#7f8ba6, 5.5:1 on navy and 4.6:1 on raised) for captions and timestamps. **Ghost Ink** (#36425c) is decoration only, never text.
- **Hairline** (rgba(150,175,225,0.12)), **Hover Hairline** (0.22) and **Strong Hairline** (0.34) separate surfaces and outline controls. **Hover Wash** (rgba(150,175,225,0.08)) fills hovered ghost buttons and rows. **Scrim** (rgba(3,6,12,0.66)) sits behind sheets.

### Named Rules
**The One Action Rule.** Holo cyan is the only colour that says "do this". Each view has one primary button. Game colours never fill a button.

**The Foil Stays On The Cards Rule.** Foil lives in the tilt sweep across a card photo and in the carousel progress bar, and nowhere else. Never on text, buttons, panels or backgrounds.

**The Colour Line Rule.** Every game keeps its hub accent as a dot beside its name, wherever the game appears. The dot never stands alone.

**The State By Form Rule.** Anything still open is dashed: a held payment, a waiting offer, an order on the way, an auction's time left, an open booth. Closed money that no longer counts is struck through. Colour is never the only signal.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Unbounded (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Onest (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo)

**Character:** Unbounded's wide, rounded geometry gives the store an arcade-signage voice for titles and prices. Onest keeps reading quiet and legible at small sizes. JetBrains Mono makes numbers feel like a live market feed.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, 64px/68px; 38px/42px on phones): a game hub's title. Page titles use 700 at 44px/50px (26px/32px on phones).
- **Headline** (600, 28px/34px; 22px/28px on phones): card names in the carousel and on listings, and titles inside flows.
- **Title** (600, 17px/22px, 0.04em, uppercase; 15px/20px on phones): section titles, the store convention. Game capsule names use Unbounded 700 at 17px/22px (14px/18px on phones).
- **Price** (Unbounded with tabular figures: 700 at 36px/40px on listings and the carousel, 600 at 24px/30px in previews and on phones, 600 at 16px/22px in tiles): money is the loudest type after a card's name.
- **Body** (400, 15px/22px; 500 for navigation): default copy, kept to 65–75ch in paragraphs. Phone inputs are 16px so iOS does not zoom.
- **Label** (700, 11px, 0.06em, uppercase): chips. Ticket stubs use JetBrains Mono 600 at 12px with 0.12em tracking, uppercase. Rail and footer headings use Unbounded 600 at 12px, uppercase.

### Named Rules
**The One Uppercase Heading Rule.** Section titles are the only uppercase headings. Chips, stubs and rail headings are the uppercase labels. Never put a card name in an uppercase title, because "ex" becomes "EX", a different card. Put names in the line beside the title.

**The Numbers Are Data Rule.** Every price, count and countdown uses tabular figures. Ticker prices, countdowns, card numbers, booth labels, and order and tracking numbers are set in JetBrains Mono.

## Layout

On web, content sits on a 1440px canvas with an 80px gutter, giving a 1280px content width. Pages stack in this order: the announcement bar (44px, while one is live), the global bar (60px), and the store bar (52px, on store pages only: home, games, hubs, browse, listings, the price guide, events, announcements, shops and seller pages). Home and the price guide add the price ticker (40px). Account, checkout, selling and admin pages show the global bar alone.

The web home pairs a 220px sticky rail with a main column 40px away. Sections sit 56px to 80px apart. Shelves auto-fill with tiles of at least 172px (five-up in the home column), and game capsules auto-fill at 220px or wider. Side panels have fixed widths: the preview panel 380px, filters 280px, order summaries 400px. Main columns take the rest.

Below 1100px the rail hides. Below 960px the carousel stacks its art above its details (the back card hides), the events block stacks, and the global and store bars scroll sideways. Main and side columns stack whenever they no longer fit.

Phones (390px) use a 16px gutter and 32px between sections. The home screen opens with the 56px header, then the phone store bar (a 48px search pill and a row of store chips, 116px in all), the ticker and the featured track. The featured track is a row of 318px capsules, 12px apart, so the next one peeks. Shelves scroll sideways with snap and 16px scroll padding. The 72px tab bar sits on the home indicator's safe area, and pages leave 96px at the bottom.

Spacing runs on a 4px step: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64 and 80px. Touch targets are at least 44px. Chips and small buttons keep their visual size and extend their hit areas to 44px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal at rest: Night Navy, then Night Panel, then Night Raised, with a hairline border on every panel. Shadows appear only on things lifted off the page: a hovered or tilted tile, menus, the notification panel, sheets, toasts, and the fanned card photos in a hub hero. Every shadow is tinted to the night ground. When a surface lifts, its border drops. Game capsules may tint their lift shadow with their own accent; nothing glows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 22px 44px -20px rgba(2,6,14,0.85)`): a card photo lifting under the pointer.
- **Fan** (`box-shadow: 0 28px 48px -18px rgba(2,6,14,0.8)`): card photos fanned in a hub hero or a game capsule.
- **Pop** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 48px -12px rgba(2,6,14,0.75)`): menus, the notification panel, sheets and toasts.

### Named Rules
**The Border Or Shadow Rule.** A surface has a border or a shadow, never both. Panels use borders at rest; lifted things trade theirs for a shadow.

**The No Glow Rule.** No neon or coloured outer glows, and no glowing buttons. Light belongs to the foil on the cards.

## Shapes

The shapes are rounded but never soft. Every small control is a pill (999px): buttons, chips, filter chips, search fields, segmented controls and avatars. Inputs use 10px corners. Card wells and tiles use 12px, panels, game capsules and event tickets 16px, and the carousel capsule and phone sheets 20px. Card photos always keep 63:88 with `object-fit: contain`.

Some forms recur. Event tickets have a date stub, a dashed tear line and punched notches. Shop avatars are rounded squares and collector avatars are round. Each game capsule carries its game's pattern (Pokémon dots, One Piece rope stripes, Basketball court rings, Lorcana facets, Magic mana dots, Yu-Gi-Oh! pips, a dot grid for the rest) and a 5px colour band along its bottom edge. A floor plan sits on a blueprint grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** a pill (999px), 44px tall by default; 40px (with a 44px hit area), 48px and 52px for headers, phones and checkout.
- **Primary:** Holo Cyan fill (#40e2e7) with Deep Teal Ink text (#04181a), Onest 600 at 15px, padding 0 20px, and a 1px white highlight along the top inside edge.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover moves to Bright Cyan (#7aeef1) over 180ms. Focus is a 2px Holo Cyan outline, 3px out. A press scales to 0.97 over 160ms.
- **Ghost / Quiet / Danger:** ghost buttons have a Hover Hairline border over a translucent panel and wash on hover. Quiet buttons are text only. Danger buttons have a Coral border and sit apart from primary actions.

### Chips
- **Style:** 22px pills, Onest 700 at 11px, 0.06em, uppercase, with a 1px border in the status line colour and no fill. Graded chips add a faint gold wash.
- **State:** open states are dashed. Filter chips are 36px pills with a 44px hit area; selected ones fill with Starlight Ink and read Night Navy.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px panels, 12px card wells.
- **Background:** Night Panel (#121a29) on Night Navy; on a hub, `skin-surface`.
- **Shadow Strategy:** a border at rest, Lift when hovered (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px Hairline (rgba(150,175,225,0.12)).
- **Internal Padding:** 16px to 24px. Listing tiles have no box: a card well, then name, condition, set, price and a meta line with the game dot.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 48px tall, 10px corners, Night Panel fill, a Hover Hairline border, 16px text, with the label above and helper text below.
- **Focus:** the border turns Holo Cyan.
- **Error / Disabled:** a Coral border and an inline error under the field that says what went wrong and how to fix it. Disabled controls sit at 45% opacity and keep their labels.

### Navigation
- **Global bar** (60px, Chrome): the wordmark, then Store, Games, Price guide, Events and Shops in Onest 500 at 15px, Dim Ink turning Starlight on hover. The current page is a Night Raised pill. Watchlist, notifications, avatar and Sell a card sit on the right.
- **Store bar** (52px, Night Panel): Browse all, New and trending, Under market, Ending soon, Graded slabs and Sealed, each with an icon, and a pill search on the right.
- **Rail** (220px): 36px rows that wash on hover, grouped under small uppercase Unbounded headings. Games show their dot and live count.
- **Phone:** the 56px header, the store bar (search pill and store chips), and the 72px tab bar: Store, Search, Sell (a Holo Cyan pill), Inbox and Me. The current tab is Starlight with a 2px cyan line on top.

### Holo Tilt (signature)
A card photo leans toward the pointer and foil light sweeps across it. A 4×4 grid of hover zones tilts the card up to 7°, lifts it 6px and scales it to 1.025 over 320ms, moving the foil highlight to that corner. On hover the well's border drops and Lift takes over. It runs on fine pointers only. On touch, a press runs one 650ms sweep with a 0.98 press. It appears on listing tiles, the preview panel and the carousel's front card, but never on a listing page's photos, where buyers inspect condition. In code it is the existing TiltCard pointer handler with this glare.

### Featured Carousel (signature)
The store's featured capsule (20px corners, 460px tall on web). On the left, the card photo blurs into a backdrop behind the front card (in Holo Tilt) and its back. On the right are the name, the game line and set, condition, price, the recent sales range, the seller, View listing and Watch, and thumbnails. Below sit a progress bar per slide (the current one fills with foil over 5 seconds), the "1 of 5" count and a pause button. On phones the capsules sit side by side in a swipeable track. ### Price Ticker
A 40px Chrome band of price moves: the game dot, the card, its price in JetBrains Mono and its 30-day change with an arrow. A loop takes 60 seconds. It pauses on hover and focus, and its pause button stops it. Under reduced motion it stands still.

### Deal Block
A 26px pill: "−12%" in Deep Teal Ink on solid Mint, then "vs median ₱24,400" on a mint wash. Tiles use the short form and move the median to the meta line. It appears only when a price is below the 30-day median of paid Atlas sales for the same card, condition or grade, with at least five sales.

### Game Capsule
A 128px capsule (88px on phones) in its game's skin. It has a glow of the game's accent in the top corner, the game's pattern, the name in Unbounded, the live count, and a 5px colour band. On hover it lifts 4px with a shadow tinted to the game's accent.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep Holo Cyan (#40e2e7) as the only action colour, with one primary button per view.
- **Do** put the game's dot beside its name in every mixed context, and give hubs their full skin.
- **Do** measure deals against the 30-day median of paid Atlas sales ("−12% vs median ₱24,400"), and only show one when at least five such sales exist.
- **Do** give anything that moves by itself a pause button, and stop it under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** show card photos at 63:88 with `object-fit: contain`, unlit on the listing page.
- **Do** dash open states and strike closed money, and pair every status colour with a word or an icon.
- **Do** keep touch targets at 44px, letting chips and small buttons extend their hit areas instead of growing.
- **Do** draw icons as SVG on a 24px grid at a 1.75 stroke, with arrows and chevrons drawn rather than typed.
- **Do** set every price, count and countdown in tabular figures.

### Don't:
- **Don't** copy Steam's colours, logo, type, iconography or assets. Borrow store structure only.
- **Don't** let game colours disappear outside their hubs, and don't fill a button with one.
- **Don't** put foil on text, buttons, panels or backgrounds.
- **Don't** show struck-through "was" prices, percentage-off-asking prices or invented discounts.
- **Don't** recolour, crop or skin a card photo or a card well.
- **Don't** put a kicker or eyebrow above a heading. A campaign sits in a meta row under its title.
- **Don't** build rows of hero-metric stat tiles. Counts live in navigation, money in one balance panel.
- **Don't** combine a border and a shadow on one surface, and don't make anything glow.
- **Don't** put a card name in an uppercase section title.
- **Don't** use emoji, typed arrow glyphs or pure black (#000) in the interface.
- **Don't** show invented counts, ratings or trends. A section with no real data is hidden.
